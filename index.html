about

Pragmatic full-stack developer with an e-commerce specialty.

I'm a full-stack developer based in Izmir, Türkiye, with 8+ years building web and mobile products — and 4+ of those years deep in the Salesforce Commerce Cloud ecosystem. As a Certified SFCC developer at OSF Global I shipped B2C solutions for brands like Berluti, Ford, Kaltire, Dalenys, Stonewall Kitchen and Rivadis Labs. Today I'm a senior backend engineer at Sorsware, working on e-commerce infrastructure and AI orchestration.

My day-to-day spans the whole stack — React and Next.js on the front, Node.js, Express, NestJS and Laravel on the back, with PostgreSQL or MongoDB underneath and Docker / GitHub Actions / AWS keeping things shipping. I lean into headless commerce, payments integrations, and clean, maintainable code.

Outside of client work I build my own products — Dinestan (a digital education platform) and Onlineantrenor (a fitness app for trainers) — and I'm always happy to chat about commerce, mobile, or anything React-shaped.
